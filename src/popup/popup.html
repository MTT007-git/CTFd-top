CTFd Top Tracker
—

The extension only runs on sites you activate here.

Exceptions

Some CTFd installs remove the “Powered by CTFd” footer link. Adding the site here treats it as CTFd and activates it.

    Settings

    Refetches automatically a moment after CTFd marks a challenge solved, so badges, counts and progress stay current without a page reload.

    Also enables the sort & group widget on the challenges page.

    Adds a button that cycles all → solved → unsolved.

    Your own progress across the challenges on the page.

    Track a single player, ignoring top-N and the watch list.

      While focusing, the challenges page gets a ⇄ Compare button that leaves only the challenges exactly one of you has solved.
